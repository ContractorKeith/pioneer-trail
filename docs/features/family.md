# Family events

Configured parties receive deterministic ages, sex, traits, and relationships. A seeded family
roll may create one eligible mother's pregnancy, due in 60–130 days. Each completed travel, rest,
wait, or lost day resolves due pregnancies once. A living mother gives birth only when the party
has room; newborns join as `Baby 1`, `Baby 2`, and so on with age 0 and health 80. A doctor halves
the 10% chance of childbirth complications.

Family content uses generic event conditions and effects. Relationship changes choose the current
best live adult pair for positive changes and lowest live adult pair for negative changes. Wedding
events record the actual pair's names as a celebration, without modeling a legal system. A member
who leaves is the lowest-morale non-leader adult; dead members and the leader remain recorded.
