# Trading trains and wagon repairs

Three named emigrants travel on serialized mileage and day schedules. A train can be traded with or invited only while present; a joined traveler may be dismissed at camp. When a recurring train returns after an absence, the journey reports that it has drawn near again.

Talk quotes require every listed season, landmark, and state tag. Quotes without any context are used only when no contextual quote fits.

At a mandatory wheel, axle, or tongue event, Repair spends a camp day. A spare part resolves the break. Without one, a blacksmith has an 80 percent chance without tools; other parties require tools and use their best living repair skill. A failed repair leaves the event open unless the party has failed.
