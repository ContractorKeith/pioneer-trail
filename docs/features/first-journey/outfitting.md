# First-journey outfitting

The Independence store has a read-only `?` advice view. It explains the unit and current
price of the selected item, calculates food days from the living party and current ration
level, and gives an affordable starter target using the live store price.

The target prefers three oxen yokes, food, one clothing set per traveler, ten ammunition
boxes (twenty rounds per box), a wheel, axle, tongue, and medicine. Food is increased toward
1,200 lb when the occupation's cash allows it while retaining a small cash reserve; lower-cash
starts receive a smaller target that still fits their actual era price. It is guidance only:
it neither purchases supplies nor alters starting cash or balance.

Choosing Depart with a thin food reserve or another basic risk shows one caution and asks for
a second deliberate selection. The player may still leave with any legal loadout.
